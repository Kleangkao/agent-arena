# Continuous Squad Arena

The 27 November owner amendment adds three playable modes to the same top-down 5v5 Arena. The original master specification and archived authoritative recordings remain preserved.

## Rules and movement

The previous Relay engine stored `at` as an integer cell index and resolved one orthogonal hop per tick. CSS interpolation smoothed those steps in the viewer. New matches lock `squads-v3` and one of `bomb`, `tdm`, or `relay` on the server. V7 replays include that configuration and use their original rules when loaded.

Positions have 1/256-world-unit precision. Three increments of up to 64 fixed-point units per 320 ms snapshot can place a unit between navigation centers. The grid routes waypoints and defines walls and cover. Body separation, arrival, shot range, wall intersection and site membership use world geometry. The renderer uses authoritative world positions. Grid-aligned routing remains visible at turns. It gives clear routes and predictable wall clearance under the current map geometry. The game can take a detour after blocked movement. Same-team claims rotate priority, while opposing proposals can remain contested. The first Bomb practice stalled before reaching either site because two allies entered the same route edge and both backed out. Reserving the edge earlier made the same scripted practice activate at North and finish.

- **Bomb Mode.** Blue can link either fictional Core site. Sand defends and can disable the active site. A stationary authorized linker needs three undamaged ticks, then the countdown lasts ten ticks. Three undamaged disable ticks win for Sand. If Blue fails to activate before the deadline, Sand wins. An activation at the deadline may finish its countdown beyond it. Reaching a site alone grants no activation or disable.
- **Team Deathmatch.** The teams race to eight eliminations. At tick 48 the higher score wins, with equal scores drawing. Units return to an available spawn after at least four ticks and hold pending a fresh order. The model chooses which units may pulse. The engine handles eligible target selection and simultaneous damage. Cover halves incoming pulse damage.
- **Relay (current, `squads-v4`).** North and South remain on the same mirrored map. A side claims a neutral or rival site after three uncontested ticks inside its 3×3 zone (0.96 seconds). Multiple occupants confer no capture bonus. Both teams in the zone pause capture and scoring. A vacant site or the owner alone clears unfinished capture progress. An uncontested owned site grants its owner one point each tick, including when empty and until a recapture completes. On the transfer tick the new owner scores. A team wins on elimination, reaching 60 points with the higher score, or leading at tick 72 (23.04 seconds of playback). Ties draw. These bounds leave up to six decision windows for redistribution. Relay now requires explicit pulse permissions.
- **Historical Relay (`squads-v1`–`squads-v3`).** Uncontested occupation scores toward 24 under the original bounds and automatic arena pulses. Historical replay transcripts, the public Commander #2933 evidence and its Testnet commitment retain the original rules.

Play opens on Bomb Mode and offers all three guest practice modes. Arena has the same selector when live squad service is enabled. Same-setup rematches lock the previous server configuration, mode, rival coaching and model. Bundled recordings and guest practice use local replay playback. One Gemini call per commander per checkpoint covers the whole team. Decisions run concurrently from the same snapshot. A match uses four ordinary windows, with an optional fifth for a late Bomb countdown. Strict validation, timeouts and retry limits still apply. Practice is available for 5v5. Local deterministic fixtures also cover 3v3, 10v10 and 20v20.
