# Gameplay contract — V2.6

Humans coach. AI competes. This document applies to the local arcade-v2.6 rules and replay v6. Historical rules and V1 verification retain their own formats.

- The world advances in 100 integer units at 3,000 units per second. Positions use 5,940 units per tile. Movement and spatial ranges use Euclidean distance. Terrain and fighter footprints retain conservative square sweeps. Grid routing provides waypoints only.
- Inputs combine optional movement, posture, facing, jump, attack, reload and Core channels. Receipts identify rejected channels. Facing has no effect on perception or combat. Crouching applies crouched speed. Airborne motion does not lock steering. Precision requires a grounded stationary state. Reload and firing are exclusive.
- Passive visibility uses current center distance, a clear terrain ray and the perception profile. Historical contact retains its original sample time. Only current legitimate contact permits targeted attack initiation. Equipment eligibility uses separate reach and resource checks. Due attacks resolve from the post-movement world and equipment rules. Losing awareness alone does not cancel an attack already authorized.
- Apply compatible inputs, move simultaneously, resolve effects from one snapshot, apply damage, settle the mode, respawn, then publish observations. Public phase and scores are separate from private activity. A life change resets its actions and controller plan.
- Core channels lock movement, jump, posture, attack and reload. Explicit cancellation resets progress and unlocks the next boundary. Timings remain 19.36s pending, 8s active and 3s per interaction. Score defaults to first to five, 75s and 1.2s respawn.
- Strategic plans authorize bounded packets and conditional alternatives. The executor uses filtered observations and shared validation. Guards, completion, interruption and lease expiry are checked on each boundary. An observation can switch the authored branch immediately. Returning to a previously sent branch renews movement, and its other channels require an eligible explicit repeat. Public map geometry and current-contact equipment reach are distinct from own action availability. Provider or exhausted-budget interruption gives no winner or learning credit. Replays reconstruct authoritative events with zero inference.
- The registry records provisional values, comparison evidence and field bounds. Historical formats do not inherit V2.6 changes.
