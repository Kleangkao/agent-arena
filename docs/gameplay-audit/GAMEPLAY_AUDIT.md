# AGENT ARENA V2.5 gameplay audit

The active world runs at 30 Hz with continuous fixed-point coordinates. The largest responsiveness limitations occur between the world and its controllers. Attack legality relies on passive visibility even when an agent has just obtained a legitimate focused observation. Model planning is limited to mostly tile-centered destinations and sampled at intervals longer than the lifetime of retained contact. These constraints explain several apparent non-responses without attributing intent to a model. [Executive summary](AUDIT_EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md)

## Scope and evidence

Reviewed the original master, execution plan, approved V2 direction, and V2.3–V2.5 amendments. Traced manual, scripted and protected model paths, the historical loaders, modes, renderer and stored results. Ran 100 existing tests with the local Hardhat RPC, eight stored reconstruction checks, 144 freshly simulated matches and separate state/adapter probes. Inspected both browser shots. The sample includes precise adversarial snapshots and reachable complete matches. No gameplay, provider, deployment, or chain changes. The fixture provider is an injected local function. ## Movement, geometry, and outcome

- Positions remain integer world coordinates. Blue starts at (8910, 20790), reaches (9210, 20790) on the first walk tick, redirects to (9210, 20490) on the second, and stops there. Eight steering directions share an L1 budget. A direct move can have an off-center destination. Route fallback uses cardinal cell-center BFS, with per-mode 2–4 edges for slow/run moves. A blocked candidate yields zero movement for that boundary and clears motion. No physical vertical state. Jump commits a bounded segment and remains hittable.
- Obstacles are four walls and four crates, each a full cell. Movement tests the segment against rectangles expanded by the 900-unit fighter half-width. Agent collision tests the relative swept square with 1800-unit half-extent. Reproduced 900/899 boundary distinction, wall block, legal reroute and two crossing paths. Exact painting/contact is closed-boundary geometry. The Phaser dot is a visual marker, with a square footprint only in debug. A physical height model, sliding, richer collision response and multi-agent collision were outside this ruleset.
- Each side has 100 HP, magazine 4 and one abstract shared attack profile. Standard consumes a round and deals 18 on hit, precision deals 34, both use the same 770-ms nominal due and no separate post-resolution cooldown. Ammo is charged at acceptance even when the shot later misses. Both attacks resolve from the previous positions with newly applied posture/motion before movement, with simultaneous damage and HP floor zero. The roll uses seed, due and mirrored side lane. Near threshold is two tiles; standard uses 76/60 and precision 40/30, with posture/motion modifiers. At the exact 4-tile radius a viable hit still counts while the opposing fighter walks 300 units farther within that frame. That frame holds the farther position and contact-loss events. [Complete parameter values](GAMEPLAY_PARAMETER_REGISTRY.md) · [Action matrix](ACTION_COMPATIBILITY_MATRIX.md)
- The own-action matrix separates overlap of existing independent timers from the one-command input boundary. Reload/Observe can begin in either order. Low-speed movement can continue during those and standard attack. Precision/Peek require stationary setup. Running and jumping prohibit new attacks and active survey, but still retain passive perception. Changing posture is blocked while in crouch motion; entry into walk preserves posture, making crouched posture at full walk speed possible. One timing model converts V2.2 values (110 ms native) to 30 Hz with completion on or after the due. Reported nominal and actual boundary times differ. [Legacy lineage](LEGACY_CONSTRAINTS.md)

## Perception, schedules, and non-response

Passive visibility uses continuous Manhattan distance and an unexpanded opaque rectangle ray, with no facing and no nav adjacency. Active Observe/Peek can expand the acquisition radius for one boundary, then contact expires to last seen for 2.2 seconds. Passive changes from target crouch and temporary run exposure can affect attack eligibility or outcome independently of nominal attack range. Attack legality and hit resolution never consult the focused sample. The diagnostics confirm a legit observed target in range at 17,920 units with Attack absent and the sample downgraded on the next tick. Separately the seed-1 far hit turns into a miss when the rival enters crouch at the same position.

World permissions are rechecked each step. Actual model plans are separate: fixed checkpoints, narrow grid catalog, some deferred picks, expiration on checkpoint/defeat, no refresh on Core/respawn/contact. Model access to own timing/feedback is incomplete. The audited prompt omits direct hidden rival resources and private world fields, while the spectator shows both sides. See pipeline and evidence limitations. [AI interface](AI_INTERFACE_AUDIT.md)

Development Seek/Roam branches have access to actual own movement/path and deterministic config seed and phase, unlike the model prompt, which receives its own position/mode and public map. Old policies decide at nominal 440ms intervals, defer while moving most phases, favor distinct reposition profiles, and treat visible/last seen differently by role and mode. Score optionally uses central pursuit, different reload thresholds and Roam stops for Precision. Core controller can prioritize interactions, has a separate optional early contest setting and returns null while channeling despite other compatible engine operations. These are deliberate benchmark constraints, useful as reference cohorts. Their results cannot establish AI ability or the balance of all policies.

## Mode-specific coordination

**Objective Duel.** Roles are assigned in locked config independently of side. Default pending 19.36s, active 8s, 3s each activate/disable, fixed Core position (26730,20790) and radius 4000. Existing low-speed movement and Observe can coexist with a channel. Explicit cancel, incompatible input, exit or defeat clears progress. Only contestable preset adds cancellation on actual damage. World events and defeat precede completion; successful legal interaction at exact deadline precedes timeout. Pending both fall draws, otherwise survivor wins, full pending timeout is Defender. Active Defender defeat wins Activator, including simultaneous defeats, and solo Activator defeat leaves the Core active. Core disable or completion settle the result. Public state exposes role, site, progress and phase. Existing V2.5 benchmark measured 162/320 Activator in default controlled cohort, Blue 82 and Sand 80 out of 160 activations by role assignment; alternate early Defender pressure shifted outcomes. The existing 64 mirror pairs preserve geometry, phase and result under mirrored seed; that is sampled side fairness. Exact historical reference still uses earlier corpse behavior.

**Score Deathmatch.** Shared world, live-only update. The wrapper scores all new defeats before target/clock checks, so simultaneous points can cause a draw and target settlement wins priority over clock. Default first to 5, 75s, respawn 1.2s if nonterminal. Fresh fighter gets 100/4 and clears movement, survey, reload, firing, exposure and contact. Only the reserve’s fixed identity and match context persist; both V2.5 memory revisions currently zero. Home plus mirrored alternates avoid live overlap; one follow-up step accepts input. Perception recomputes from the new state even though respawn is appended after world contact processing. The browser ordinary-score recording reaches Sand 5–4 at tick 1622. The server queue clears at defeat, without replacing it at respawn.

**Elimination.** Shared space and arcade mechanics in a simple bounded 19.36s round. Result on defeat or greater health at clock, equal health/double defeat draw. Terminal clears activity and rejects further frames. Explicit V2.5 reasons accompany the legacy world semantics. No protected V2.5 model route for this mode.

## Replay and cost

Canonical V5 validation reconstructs initial state, ordered event list, time, transcript, and final state from its versioned config. It rejects tampered future events, input suffixes and result changes. Browser reads elapsed simulation time and interpolates adjacent authorized positions while displaying the latest available frame. Score respawn does not get an ordinary adjacent-position tween. The browser verified displayed score, timing and winner, refreshed to the recorded initial state, and generated zero API requests or page errors. Historical compatibility passed the existing staged tests. Interpolation does not carry sub-tick attack coordinates and event effects can theoretically skip under multi-tick batching. The browser did not force a stall to confirm the latter. [Browser evidence](AUDIT_EVIDENCE.md)

The 48-match cohorts took roughly 2.1–3.4 ms median for complete headless generation on this host with Node 24. Replay size averaged 5.4 KB Objective, 7.8 KB elimination, 18.4 KB Score. Ordinary Score reconstructed in about 10 ms in Node; client runs full reconstruction on import and retains all frames. A one-second desktop rAF sample measured 241 callbacks over 1004 ms; it measures scheduling on this desktop only, lacks CPU/memory profiling, and is separate from Phaser FPS. Full mode inputs and geometry are suitable for continued 1v1. Larger maps, more agents, private state expansion or more equipment would need pressure tests, not an engine migration decision now. The coupled imported single equipment constants and lack of equipment identity in V5 mean alternate fictional profiles would require deliberate config and rules versioning. ## Owner decisions

- **What kind of contact supports an attack?** Current passive-only behavior is simple internally but contradicts the intuitive reading of current focused contact. Recommend treating current authorized contact and arcade eligibility as explicit separate values, and evaluating a rule that permits focused current contact with clear world geometry. A stricter rule is defensible if clearly described and rendered. Last seen should remain distinguishable from current contact in either case.
- **What does crouched walking mean?** Retain posture and gait independently with explicit effects, or make crouched locomotion select its own pace. Recommend one coherent crouched pace by default because the manual controls already imply that distinction. This affects both perception and movement balance.
- **What resolves first at the same boundary?** Current attack sees new posture, old position. Favor a single documented sampling convention, with deterministic symmetric handling and evidence for range crossing. A ruleset-level ordering change needs new replay fixtures and rerun of balance.
- **How much opponent Core activity is public?** Role, phase and progress are currently public and reveal the actor is in the site. Recommend keeping the public competitive phase/clock; decide whether opposing channel progress should be public, or available only in contact. Keep the choice explicit before widening observed-event prompts.

Technical decisions about test fixtures, stable replay schemas, logging and prompt units need no owner input. Genuine controller behavior, low-end hardware, manual collision-release and replay batching still need evidence.
